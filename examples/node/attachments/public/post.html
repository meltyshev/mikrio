Reusable POST demonstration

Sign in to the attachment app, then return here.

This separate private bucket holds temporary demonstration files. A form can replace its object repeatedly until it expires. Files are never attached to an application record.

The same form and key are reused when you choose another file. A browser response, redirect, or CORS failure may happen after publication; use readback to check the object.

Deletion does not revoke the form. Recurring cleanup removes expired demo objects and discovers later writes. This page makes no promise of final absence.
